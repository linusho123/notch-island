# User needs

Why notch-island exists, need by need, and what actually covers each one.
The ledger explains and prioritizes; it never specifies — at build only the
feature files bind, and a `partial` status is a ratified stopping point, not
debt. Ids are the docket's (`DOCKET.md`). The visionary is every stakeholder
here: the island is for their own MacBook Air. Weights for N1, N2, N4, N5 and
N6 were confirmed at the intent readback; N7, N8 and N9 are the interviewer's
guesses, accepted as drafted; N10's weight of two was put after the cold
read and accepted.

- **N0** (visionary, wt 5) — *I need a module for my own MacBook Air, built so others might install it later, that turns the dead space around the camera notch into something useful: hovering over it gives me the current song and its controls, faster than clicking through the menu bar; it pops out by itself when a new song starts; and it holds a quick-access shelf.* Evidence: D9, D95, D96 — the confirmed vision, the four behaviour areas and their actors, the end-to-end readback that found nothing missing, and the electives offer answered "make it so". Coverage: `scenario` — all six feature files (music-panel, music-sources, new-song-notice, shelf-captures-and-files, shelf-notes-and-reminders, around-the-clock); the root vision every other need ladders from, carried through N1, N2, N5, N6, N7 and N9.
- **N1** (visionary, wt 5) — *I need to control my music faster than clicking the play button in the menu bar.* Means, ruled: hovering the trackpad pointer over the notch shows the current song with pause or resume, skip and back. Evidence: D3, D4, D5, D16, D18, D19, D20, D24, D25, D26, D28, D29, D31, D46, D47, D48, D51, D52, D60, D68, D69, D74, D76, D86, D88, D90, D99, D100, D107, D111, D112, D114, D117, D119, D120, D121, D122, D124, D125, D126, D137, D140 — the sources followed and the companion Chrome extension, the hover and its timings, the panel and its controls, the source-line labels, several sources at once with their count, reopened sources resuming where they left off, the island showing the app's real state, and the controls that already work kept working. Coverage: `scenario` — music-panel.feature, music-sources.feature.
- **N2** (visionary, wt 4) — *I need to see what is playing when a new song starts, without doing anything.* Means, ruled: the notch pops out by itself when a new song begins, as a single wing left of the notch that never covers a menu or status icon and never reaches right of the notch (D113, D114). Evidence: D3, D4, D5, D16, D28, D44, D78, D88, D107, D113, D114, D115, D138, D139 — the wing, its four seconds, rapid skipping, the no-room cases, and the one-second bar on every followed source. Tension: N5, N10 (see Tensions). Coverage: `partial` — new-song-notice.feature; the pop-out never shows on the external monitor, only at the built-in notch, is off in fullscreen, does not pop when the left gap cannot hold the cover art, and is silent for muted video and for a preview being unmuted. The external-monitor loss is the accepted trade-off capped by N5, and the fullscreen loss the one capped by N10; the weight was reaffirmed at four despite the external-monitor loss (see Tensions).
- **N3** (visionary, wt 3) — *I need a quick-access shelf in the notch.* Evidence: D29 — the shelf as a page of the hover panel. Coverage: `scenario` — the files of N6. Note: a means wearing a need's clothing, picked without a why; laddered in Phase 1 into N6, which amends it and carries every shelf ruling since.
- **N4** (visionary, wt 2) — *I need other people to be able to install this on their own Macs, maybe, someday.* Evidence: D82 — sharing is a maybe, and no behaviour depends on it. Coverage: `fenced` — install-and-update, deferred until the visionary decides to share the island (OUT-OF-SCOPE.md, Deferred).
- **N5** (visionary, wt 4) — *I want the space around the camera put to use for something useful instead of sitting dead.* Evidence: D16, D52, D60, D70, D86, D136, D137, D139, D140, D145 — the island lives only at the built-in notch, closes after a short grace, is there at every login, can be quit from itself, and leaves no dead zone around the closed notch. Tension: N2 (see Tensions). Coverage: `scenario` — new-song-notice.feature, around-the-clock.feature. Note: also structural — the island occupies only the dead space around the notch and the menu bar strip beside it, never a menu or status icon (DESIGN.md); no scenario can assert that the space feels used rather than dead.
- **N6** (visionary, wt 3) — *I need a quick place to park things I am moving or want to keep in mind: screenshots and files I want to drag somewhere else quickly, ideas in super short note form, and reminders.* Means, ruled: a quick-access shelf in the notch. Evidence: D2, D29, D30, D32, D33, D34, D36, D37, D39, D40, D52, D57, D58, D59, D61, D62, D63, D64, D65, D66, D76, D89, D90, D92, D93, D98, D102, D103, D104, D105, D106, D116, D142, D143 — captures landing on their own, dropped files, folders and links, notes and reminders, removal, ageing out, restarts, drags that are refused, and a drop on the Trash in the Dock moving what an item points at to the Trash. Coverage: `scenario` — shelf-captures-and-files.feature, shelf-notes-and-reminders.feature.
- **N7** (visionary, wt 3) — *I need the island to cost my fanless Air almost nothing while it sits there, aiming to barely register and never costing more than a typical menu bar utility.* Evidence: D79 — the ceiling of three percent CPU and an Energy Impact of five over an idle minute. Coverage: `scenario` — around-the-clock.feature. Note: the scenario holds the ceiling, a typical menu bar utility; the near-zero aim is prose only, and no scenario asserts it.
- **N8** (visionary, wt 2) — *I need to be able to add new music sources and new uses of the island later without disturbing what already works.* Evidence: D80, D141 — each source and each use is a separate part; an AI integration is noted as a future use, not scoped. Coverage: `structural` — separate parts per source and per use (DESIGN.md). A new use may one day be one of the uses declined in other-island-uses; this need does not reopen that.
- **N9** (visionary, wt 3) — *I need my notes, reminders and shelf to stay off other people's screens when I share or record mine.* Evidence: D108, D109, D144 — while sharing or recording, the island keeps working for me while nothing of it appears in what the share or the recording captures, resting on the named assumption hide-from-capture (OUT-OF-SCOPE.md). Coverage: `scenario` — around-the-clock.feature; the Zoom and Meet viewer scenarios are deferred for lack of a real viewer's evidence (share-viewer-evidence, D144), and the guarantee rests on the screen recording and system capture scenarios.
- **N10** (visionary, wt 2) — *When I am watching something fullscreen, I do not want it interrupted.* Evidence: D125 — while any app is fullscreen a new song never pops the wing, and hovering still opens the panel. Tension: N2 (see Tensions). Coverage: `scenario` — new-song-notice.feature, the fullscreen scenario "A new song in fullscreen pops no wing". Note: laddered after the cold read from the ruling it now sits under; weight two as put and accepted.

## Tensions

- Between N2 and N5: the island lives only at the built-in notch, so with eyes on the external monitor a new-song pop-out can go unseen, so the external monitor caps N2 for N5; accepted at D16, and at the adversarial pass N2 kept weight four with that loss named as a trade-off in service of N5 (D16, D91).
- Between N2 and N10: while any app is fullscreen a new song never pops the wing, though hovering still opens the panel, so a movie is not interrupted; fullscreen caps N2 in service of N10 (D125, D100).
- Between N2 and N9: no longer a tension. Sharing or recording the screen no longer silences the wing: the island keeps working for the visionary while nothing of it appears in what is captured (D108), replacing the earlier ruling under which a new song went unannounced while sharing (D83).
- Between N2 and N1's Chrome rule: a muted YouTube preview never pops the wing, and unmuting it adds it to the flip card without popping the wing either, since unmuting is doing something (D88, D107, D122).

## Candidate needs from the domain question

Asked at the close of the checklist sweep, from the interviewer's priors:
staying off other people's screens while sharing or recording, which became
N9; a way to quit or reach settings from the island, a volume slider, and a
love or like button, all declined except quitting (common-extras).
